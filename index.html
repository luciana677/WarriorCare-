WarriorCare

A mobile companion designed to help people living with chronic blood conditions, and the caregivers beside them, manage everyday health and respond calmly in a crisis.

The problem

Managing a chronic blood condition means constant tracking: symptoms, medications, warning signs and appointments. Patients often do it alone, and caregivers are left guessing when things get worse. Important details get lost between clinic visits, and in a crisis, minutes matter.

WarriorCare is being built to put patient and caregiver on the same page, every day.

What we are building

Patient + guardian dashboards

Two linked views connected by a simple invite code, so caregivers see what they need to.

Daily check-ins & logs

Quick symptom and crisis logging that builds a clear picture over time.

Medication adherence

Tracking that helps patients stay consistent with their treatment.

Risk scoring & crisis mode

Early warning signals and a focused mode for when a crisis begins.

AI health summaries

Plain-language summaries of recent history to share at clinic visits.

Support & education

Guided breathing, a peer community and trusted educational resources.
